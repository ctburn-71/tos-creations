Privacy Policy

My Music OS | TOS Creations

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

My Music OS is developed by TOS Creations. We respect your creative work and the importance of protecting your music, recordings, and personal information.

This policy describes how the current application handles imported audio, saved projects, recordings, device storage, and sharing.

Information Stored on Your Device

My Music OS stores supported application information locally to provide its DJ mixing and music organization features.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Imported track files and metadata
  • Track titles and import dates
  • Named crates and track organization
  • Deck and session settings
  • Fader, EQ, and pitch settings
  • Hot cues and loop regions
  • Coach and metronome settings
  • Theme preferences
  • Achievement identifiers
  • Recording titles, dates, and durations
  • Local file references and identifiers

These records support the application's mixing, playback, organization, and personalization features.

Imported Music

When you select an audio file using the system document picker, My Music OS copies the selected file into the application's private local storage on supported mobile devices.

Imported track information is also stored locally so you can organize and reload tracks.

The current application does not automatically upload imported music to a TOS Creations server or provide an app-operated cloud synchronization service.

Selecting a file from a cloud document provider may involve that provider's services and applicable privacy practices.

In the web version, imported file references may be temporary and may not remain available after the browser session.

Audio Analysis and Smart Sync

My Music OS performs supported audio analysis locally using application code.

For its built-in studio loops, this includes tempo estimation, musical key estimation, Camelot compatibility, and supported Smart Sync functionality.

The current implementation does not provide automatic BPM or musical-key analysis for arbitrary imported device audio files.

These analysis features do not require uploading music to an external audio-analysis service.

Detected musical characteristics are estimates and may not always be accurate.

Recording Your Mixes

My Music OS includes a recording feature for supported in-app mixes.

In the currently audited implementation, recording creates a mixdown from the application's built-in studio loops. It does not capture microphone audio.

Recording is not currently available when a device-imported track is loaded into the supported recording flow.

On supported mobile devices, finished recordings are stored as WAV files in the application's local Documents storage.

Recording metadata, including title, date, duration, and local file reference, is stored locally.

In the web version, exported recordings are downloaded through the browser rather than added to the application's saved recordings library.

Sharing and Exporting Recordings

After a recording is exported on supported mobile devices, My Music OS presents the operating system's sharing interface.

Opening the sharing interface does not itself mean that the recording has been uploaded.

You decide whether to save or share the file through a selected application or destination.

Files shared or saved through other services are subject to the privacy and storage practices of those services.

Please only share audio you own or have permission to distribute.

Accounts and Cloud Services

My Music OS does not currently require a separate TOS Creations account, login, email registration, or application password.

The audited application does not implement a custom backend for collecting music or player data.

It also does not implement an app-operated cloud synchronization service for imported tracks, recordings, crates, or achievements.

Operating systems and document providers may separately process information for backups, file access, and user-selected sharing.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The current application source and dependency audit did not identify developer-integrated advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

No app-operated service for uploading user music or recording activity was identified in the audited implementation.

Platform providers may separately process diagnostic, backup, distribution, and operational information according to their applicable privacy policies.

Device Permissions

My Music OS uses device audio capabilities for playback and mixing functionality.

On supported platforms, the app may use system file-picker and file-sharing services when requested by the user.

The currently audited configuration disables microphone recording.

Certain Android permissions support audio playback, vibration, file handling, and application platform functions.

The source audit did not identify app-configured requests for camera, location, contacts, or advertising tracking permission.

Data Storage and Security

My Music OS uses private application storage and local key-value storage to maintain its supported data.

The current implementation does not configure additional app-level database encryption.

Local data protection also depends on your device's operating system, security settings, and access controls.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Data Deletion

The application provides controls for removing supported imported tracks, crates, and recordings.

Removing an imported track or recording updates its library information and attempts to remove the associated local file.

File removal may not complete if an underlying storage error occurs.

Deleting a crate removes its organization information but does not automatically delete the tracks assigned to it.

Removing the application normally deletes its local application storage, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior.

Files previously exported or shared outside the application are not automatically removed when My Music OS is deleted.

Device Backups

Although the application does not implement its own cloud synchronization, operating-system backups may include some application data.

Apple Devices

Local application data may be included in iCloud device backups depending on device settings and platform behavior. My Music OS does not configure a separate iCloud synchronization container.

Android Devices

The audited Android configuration allows operating-system backups. Saved application files and records may be backed up or restored according to device settings and Android backup policies.

Web Version

Browser-managed storage and downloaded files are subject to the browser's storage and deletion behavior.

Children's Privacy

My Music OS is not specifically directed toward children under 13.

The current application does not require a separate user account or directly request account contact information.

For concerns involving a child's privacy, please contact TOS Creations.

Privacy Policy Updates and Beta Testing

My Music OS is currently undergoing beta testing and active development.

TOS Creations will review and update this Privacy Policy as needed when new builds introduce features or change how information is collected, stored, processed, or shared.

Before public release, TOS Creations will perform a final privacy review based on the application's actual release features and data practices.

Material changes will be reflected in this policy when they take effect. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.

The effective date will be revised when updated policies are published.
