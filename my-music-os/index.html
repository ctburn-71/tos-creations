Welcome to My Music OS Support

My Music OS transforms your phone into a dual-deck DJ booth. Load tracks, blend audio, explore harmonic mixing, and record your sets using supported on-device features.

Getting Started

Loading Tracks

Import supported audio tracks from your device and load them onto Deck A or Deck B. Organize your library using named crates to make your tracks easier to find.

Using the Crossfader

Move the crossfader between Deck A and Deck B to blend their audio. Use the center position to hear both decks.

Tempo and Musical Key Analysis

My Music OS analyzes supported audio locally to estimate tempo, musical key, and beat information. Results are estimates and may vary depending on the recording.

Camelot Harmonic Mixing Wheel

View the detected keys from both decks on the interactive Camelot wheel. Use its visual indicators to explore harmonically compatible mixes.

Smart Sync and Metronome

Use Smart Sync to align supported deck playback to a common beat grid, or use the metronome for timing assistance.

Hot Cues, Loops, and Pitch Controls

Set cue points, control loops, and adjust pitch using the available deck controls.

Recording a Set

Use the recording feature to capture your live mix, then save or share the resulting recording through the available system controls.

Only record or share audio you own or are authorized to use.

Built-In Studio Loops

Explore the five included studio loops to practice mixing without importing your own music.

Studio Coach

Use the built-in guidance to learn what each control does and become familiar with the mixing workflow.

Achievements

Complete supported mixing activities to unlock achievements and track your progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't the detected BPM or key seem correct?

Automatic tempo and key detection provides estimates. Different recordings, complex rhythms, and musical arrangements can affect the results.

What if a track won't load or play?

Check that the file is accessible and in a supported format. Verify your device's volume and audio output. If the issue continues, contact support.

Why don't my decks sound synchronized?

Check the detected tempo, playback settings, and Smart Sync controls. Some recordings may require manual adjustment.

What if a recording cannot be saved?

Check available device storage and your selected save destination. If the problem continues, report the steps you followed and any error message.

What if the app freezes or closes unexpectedly?

Please include your device model, app version, the screen you were using, and what happened before the problem.
