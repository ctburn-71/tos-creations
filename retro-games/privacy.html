Privacy Policy

TOS Retro Games | TOS Creations

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

TOS Retro Games is developed by TOS Creations. We aim to provide an enjoyable arcade experience while respecting player privacy.

This policy explains how the current game stores progress, handles device features, and interacts with operating-system services.

Locally Saved Game Information

TOS Retro Games uses local application storage to remember your gameplay information.

Depending on the features you use, saved information may include:

  • Game scores and leaderboard entries
  • Three-letter player initials
  • Selected difficulty levels
  • Selected sky and unlocked visual themes
  • Daily challenge participation and streaks
  • Personal best scores and achievement dates
  • Medals and skyline progress
  • Campaign completion records

The current implementation stores this information locally using AsyncStorage.

Sound mute and coach settings are currently managed in application memory rather than persistent game storage.

High Scores and Player Initials

TOS Retro Games maintains local high-score leaderboards for supported game levels and difficulty settings.

Players may enter three-letter initials when qualifying for a leaderboard.

These initials and scores are stored on the device. They are not currently uploaded to a developer-operated online leaderboard.

Daily Challenges and Achievements

Daily challenges are generated locally using date-based game logic.

Daily participation, streaks, medals, and related achievements may be saved in local application storage.

The current application does not download daily challenges from an external player-data server.

Accounts and Online Services

TOS Retro Games does not currently require players to create an account or provide an email address or password.

The audited application does not implement developer-operated player accounts, cloud synchronization, multiplayer services, or online score submission.

Operating systems, app stores, distribution platforms, and development services may independently process technical information under their applicable policies.

Device Features and Permissions

TOS Retro Games uses supported audio and haptic features to provide gameplay sounds and feedback.

On Android, the application may declare permissions related to audio settings, vibration, and network capability. Certain file-system permissions may also be present depending on the installed Android version.

The current source audit did not identify gameplay requests for camera, microphone recording, contacts, location, photographs, or notification access.

Declared technical permissions do not necessarily mean that personal information is being collected or transmitted.

Audio and Sound Effects

The game generates its retro-inspired audio locally.

Temporary audio cache files may be created to support mobile playback. The web version may use locally generated audio data.

The current game does not use microphone recording or upload player-recorded audio.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The current source and dependency audit did not identify developer-integrated advertising, analytics, third-party crash-reporting, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

No developer-operated service for collecting player gameplay records was identified in the audited implementation.

Platform providers may independently process diagnostic, distribution, or operational information according to their own privacy practices.

Device Backups and Storage

Although game progress is saved locally, device backup and restoration behavior may vary by operating system.

Apple Devices

The current iOS AsyncStorage implementation excludes its stored game data from iCloud backup by default.

Deleting the app normally removes its locally stored information. Offloading an app may preserve its documents and data.

Android Devices

Depending on Android backup settings, saved gameplay data may be included in operating-system backups and restored when the app is reinstalled.

Backup behavior is managed by the device operating system and may vary between devices.

Web Version

The web version uses browser-based local storage for supported game information. Clearing that storage may erase progress.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved game information generally remains available while the application and its local storage are retained.

Removing the application or clearing application data may remove locally saved scores, initials, and progress.

Some information may be restored from an operating-system backup when backup and restoration are enabled.

There is currently no separate TOS Creations account recovery or developer-operated cloud restore service for player progress.

Children's Privacy

The current game does not require player account registration or direct submission of contact details.

If you have concerns about a child's privacy while using TOS Retro Games, please contact TOS Creations.

Security

TOS Creations aims to follow appropriate practices for the technology used to operate its apps.

Locally stored data and operating-system backups are subject to device, platform, and account security settings.

No electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Privacy Updates During Beta Development

TOS Retro Games is undergoing testing and ongoing development.

TOS Creations will review and update this Privacy Policy as needed when application builds introduce features or change how information is handled.

Before public release, TOS Creations will perform a final privacy review based on the actual release build, including its permissions, storage, and network behavior.

Material changes to data practices will be reflected in this policy when those changes take effect. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.

The effective date will be revised when updates are published.
