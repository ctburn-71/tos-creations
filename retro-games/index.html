Welcome to TOS Retro Games Support

Step inside the neon grid and experience five distinct arcade challenges built around timing, balance, spatial reasoning, and rhythm.

This page provides help with gameplay, scoring, audio, controls, and reporting issues during development and testing.

Understanding the Five Levels

LEVEL 1

Classic Slice

Drop sliding blocks onto your tower. Any overhanging portion is sliced away, making the next landing platform smaller.

Focus on timing and accurate alignment to keep your tower growing.

LEVEL 2

The Balance Beam

Blocks retain their full size when they land. Their position affects the tower's center of mass.

Watch for shifting weight, wobbling, and tipping as your stack grows.

LEVEL 3

Grid-Fit Puzzle

Arrange fractional blocks inside the target area to complete a row without gaps.

Use the different piece sizes to plan each placement carefully.

LEVEL 4

The Shift Grid

Your landing area moves from side to side. Time each drop while accounting for the moving target.

LEVEL 5

Blind Neon

The landing grid and target briefly appear in rhythm with the soundtrack.

Use timing and visual memory to guide your next drop.

Scoring and Arcade Features

High Scores

Each level has its own local Top 5 leaderboard, allowing you to compete against your previous scores.

When a score qualifies for the leaderboard, enter your three-letter initials when prompted.

Streak Multipliers

Consecutive successful placements can trigger score multipliers and additional points.

Watch for streak indicators during gameplay.

Adaptive Arcade Soundtrack

TOS Retro Games uses procedurally generated retro-style audio that changes with gameplay.

Tempo and pitch may shift as your challenge becomes more demanding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my tower collapse in Level 2?

The Balance Beam uses weight distribution. Placing too much weight to one side can cause the tower to become unstable.

Why can't I complete a row in Level 3?

Grid-Fit Puzzle requires pieces to fit together without leaving gaps. Check the available piece sizes and their positions in the target area.

Why does the landing target disappear in Level 5?

Blind Neon intentionally limits how long the target remains visible. Use the brief flashes and soundtrack rhythm to time your actions.

What if my high score does not save?

Report the level, score, device model, and what happened when the round ended.

The current game description specifies local leaderboards rather than online account-based score synchronization.

What if the game freezes or closes?

Include your device model, app version, game level, and the actions leading up to the problem.

What if the soundtrack is missing?

Check your device's volume and audio output. If the issue continues, report the level and any audio settings involved.
