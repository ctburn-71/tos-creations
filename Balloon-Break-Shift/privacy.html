Balloon Breakshift

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Current Status: In Development / Testing

1. Introduction

Balloon Breakshift is a mobile puzzle game developed by TOS Creations.

We respect your privacy and aim to provide an enjoyable game while minimizing the personal information needed to play.

2. Accounts and Personal Information

Balloon Breakshift does not require players to create an account, provide an email address, or sign in to play.

The game does not currently provide account-based synchronization of progress across devices.

3. Saved Game Progress

Balloon Breakshift saves game information locally on your device so you can return to where you left off.

Saved information may include your current level, score, progress, boosters, rewards, and selected game preferences.

Depending on your device settings, operating-system backup services may also back up or restore locally saved data.

4. Daily Rewards and Boosters

The game may use your device's date information to determine daily reward eligibility.

Reward status and available boosters may be saved locally to support continued gameplay.

5. In-App Purchases

Real-money purchases are disabled in the current testing version of Balloon Breakshift.

Optional paid bonus packs may be introduced in a future release. If purchasing features become available, this policy will be updated as necessary to reflect their data-handling practices.

6. Analytics and Testing

Balloon Breakshift is developed using GDevelop. GDevelop session analytics have been disabled in the current testing build, version 1.0.0 (build 2).

Game progress, scores, rewards, and booster inventories are saved locally and are not intentionally transmitted to TOS Creations servers.

During beta testing, Apple TestFlight may collect crash reports, testing activity, device information, and feedback under Apple's applicable privacy practices. Some information may be available to TOS Creations for testing and support.

We will continue reviewing the game's data-handling practices as testing progresses and before public release.

7. Advertising and Tracking

The current beta is not designed to display third-party advertisements or use player information for personalized advertising.

TOS Creations does not intentionally sell players' personal information to advertisers.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved game progress generally remains until it is removed through app or device data controls.

Removing the application or clearing its data may permanently erase saved progress and boosters.

Device backups and information handled by testing or support providers may follow separate retention practices.

Previous testing builds included GDevelop session analytics. Disabling analytics in the current build does not automatically delete information previously processed by that service.

If you contact TOS Creations for assistance, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message.

9. Children's Privacy

Balloon Breakshift does not require personal registration information to access its core gameplay.

Parents and guardians may contact TOS Creations with questions or concerns about information relating to younger players.

10. Security

TOS Creations aims to minimize unnecessary collection of information and use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the services involved.

No electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Policy Updates

Balloon Breakshift is actively being developed and tested.

This policy may change as gameplay, testing services, and optional purchasing features evolve.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
