About Balloon Breakshift

Balloon Breakshift is a colorful, strategy-driven puzzle adventure where every match can change the game.

Match balloons, create powerful combinations, break obstacles, and explore changing challenges as you advance through levels.

Features are being developed and refined. Some described gameplay elements may not yet be available in every testing build.

How to Play

Matching Balloons

Match three or more balloons of the same color to earn points and clear spaces on the board.

Power Balloons

  • Rocket Balloon: Clears a row or column.
  • Rainbow Balloon: Clears balloons of a selected color.
  • Burst Balloon: Creates an explosion affecting nearby balloons.
  • Wild Rocket: A planned special rocket that can travel unpredictably and strike multiple targets.

Obstacles

Glass, wooden crates, stone, and reinforced metal can require different numbers of hits to break.

Changing Boards

Randomized starting layouts help create different challenges. Evolving board mechanics, including row and column collapses, are part of the game's development direction.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a power balloon?

Make larger or special matching patterns. The resulting power depends on the combination and your game version.

Why didn't my balloon swap work?

Some swaps may be invalid if they do not create an eligible match. Obstacles and special pieces may also restrict movement.

Why didn't an obstacle break?

Some obstacles require several hits. Try matching balloons beside the obstacle or using an applicable power boost.

Why is the starting board different?

Balloon Breakshift is designed to use randomized layouts so levels can offer different arrangements and challenges.

Can I continue a saved game?

The game is designed to save progress automatically. Available recovery behavior may vary during testing. Please report any lost progress.

Can I change music or sound settings?

Where available in your build, use the game's settings to adjust background music and sound effects.

Troubleshooting

If the game is not working as expected, try the following:

  1. Close and reopen Balloon Breakshift.
  2. Check that your device has enough available storage.
  3. Make sure you're using the latest testing build.
  4. Check your sound settings if audio is missing.
  5. Report any persistent errors or lost progress.

Avoid uninstalling the game before reporting a save-related problem, since local progress may be lost.

Help Us Improve Balloon Breakshift

During testing, we're especially interested in feedback about:

  • Balloon movement and matching responsiveness
  • Rocket speed, direction, and visibility
  • Explosion effects and chain reactions
  • Obstacle damage and removal
  • Starting-board variety and level difficulty
  • Booster behavior and rewards
  • Saved progress and level transitions
  • Sound, music, animation, and performance
  • Freezes, crashes, or unexpected gameplay

When reporting an issue, please include your device model, operating system, level number, what happened, and how to reproduce it. Screenshots or recordings are helpful.
