Welcome to Crossword Jigsaw Support

Crossword Jigsaw brings together word puzzles and visual discovery. Solve crossword clues to unlock jigsaw pieces and gradually reveal the hidden artwork.

Getting Started

Playing Your First Puzzle

Open Crossword Jigsaw and select an available puzzle. Follow the in-app instructions to begin solving clues and revealing the image.

Solving Crossword Clues

Read each clue and enter your answer using the available puzzle controls. Correct answers help you progress toward completing the picture.

Unlocking Jigsaw Pieces

As you solve crossword clues, additional jigsaw pieces become available, bringing you closer to revealing the complete artwork.

Choosing a Difficulty

Explore the available difficulty settings to find a puzzle experience that suits your preference and experience level.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if a clue or answer seems incorrect?

Please contact support with the puzzle name, difficulty level, the clue involved, and the answer you expected.

What if a puzzle becomes stuck?

Try returning to the puzzle using the available navigation controls. If the issue continues, contact support with details about what happened.

How do I report a display or control problem?

Tell us which screen you were using, what you attempted to do, and what occurred. Screenshots can be helpful when reporting layout or puzzle-control problems.

Beta Testing and Feedback

Crossword Jigsaw is currently being tested and refined. Feedback from testers helps us improve puzzle difficulty, controls, clarity, and overall reliability.

Please include your app version, device model, and a brief description of the issue when reporting a problem.
