Privacy Policy

Crossword Jigsaw | TOS Creations

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

Crossword Jigsaw is developed by TOS Creations. We respect the privacy of our players and aim to make our data practices understandable.

This policy explains how Crossword Jigsaw handles saved puzzle progress, downloaded content, and related information.

Puzzle Progress and Local Storage

Crossword Jigsaw saves puzzle progress locally on the player's device using application storage.

Locally saved information may include:

  • Crossword answers
  • Placed jigsaw pieces
  • Puzzle completion records
  • Timers and hints
  • Game preferences and settings
  • Master-puzzle progress

This information allows you to continue playing and maintain your puzzle progress.

Crossword Jigsaw does not currently upload this saved puzzle progress to an online progress database.

Downloaded Content and Holiday Packs

Crossword Jigsaw downloads puzzle catalogs and artwork over HTTPS. Updated versions use content hosted on the TOS Creations website at toscreations.com. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Google Cloud Storage also delivers existing artwork and the original puzzle catalog, including as a fallback when the new catalog has not yet been published.

Downloadable collections may include seasonal and holiday puzzles. The app checks for new content when it opens, returns to the foreground, or a puzzle is completed. Automatic checks are limited to once every 15 minutes while the app is running. You can also select “Check for new puzzles.” Checks are initiated while the app is in use; the app does not schedule a separate background download service.

Downloaded catalogs and artwork are saved on the device for offline play. Scheduled puzzles become available according to the device's local date. An internet connection is needed to obtain new content or replace missing artwork.

Content requests include the requested file address and may include a refresh timestamp. They do not attach a player account identifier, advertising identifier, locally entered crossword answers, puzzle progress, or saved game preferences.

Hosting providers receive technical network information needed to handle requests, including the connection's IP address. They may also process or log request times, requested URLs, client software information such as a user agent, request identifiers, and response information. GitHub Pages states that it logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Google Cloud may process service metadata for operating, securing, measuring, and improving its services under its applicable practices.

For details, see GitHub Pages data collection, the GitHub Privacy Statement, and the Google Cloud Privacy Notice. Provider-managed request logs are separate from locally saved game progress.

Accounts and Synchronization

Crossword Jigsaw does not currently require a player account.

There is currently no cross-device synchronization service for puzzle progress.

TOS Creations does not operate an account-based progress synchronization or recovery service. Device backup and restore may nevertheless transfer app data as described below.

Device Backups and Restore

Depending on the device, operating system, account, and backup settings, Android, iOS, or the device provider may include locally saved progress, preferences, and downloaded resources in device backups or device transfers. For example, Android Auto Backup or an Apple device backup may restore app data during device setup or reinstallation.

These backups and transfers are managed by the operating system or backup provider under its terms and privacy practices. TOS Creations does not receive or operate these backups. Availability and restoration depend on device settings, compatibility, storage limits, and provider policies; recovery is not guaranteed. Manage backups through your device or backup account settings.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. TOS Creations does not use the app to build advertising profiles. This does not mean that content-hosting providers keep no technical logs; their request handling is described above.

Offline Play

Previously downloaded puzzle resources may remain available for offline play.

Downloading new artwork or updated puzzle catalogs requires a network connection.

Data Retention and Deletion

Puzzle progress is stored locally and may remain available while the app's saved data is retained.

Clearing application data, removing locally stored game information, or clearing browser storage in the web version may permanently erase saved progress.

TOS Creations does not provide its own cloud-based progress recovery. Clearing app data does not necessarily remove an existing operating-system backup. Use the backup provider's controls to manage or delete those copies.

Players should not rely on progress automatically transferring between devices or surviving removal of local app data.

Hosting providers may retain technical request logs for their operational, security, and other disclosed service purposes in accordance with their published practices. TOS Creations does not set a separate retention period for those provider-managed logs. This policy does not promise that request metadata is unlogged or deleted immediately after delivery.

Information Sharing

Crossword Jigsaw does not currently send locally saved puzzle progress to an online player-progress database.

GitHub Pages and Google Cloud Storage support content delivery and may process technical request information as described above. The app's content downloads do not upload locally saved puzzle answers or progress.

Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards for the services supporting Crossword Jigsaw.

No network or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Crossword Jigsaw does not currently require players to create an account or submit personal account information.

The local-storage, content-download, and device-backup practices described in this policy also apply when children play. The current app has no in-app advertising or account-based social features. Technical hosting requests may still be processed as described above. A parent or guardian can manage the device's connectivity, app data, and backup settings.

If you have questions concerning a child's privacy while using Crossword Jigsaw, please contact TOS Creations.

Privacy Policy Updates and Beta Testing

Crossword Jigsaw is currently undergoing testing and active development.

TOS Creations will review and update this Privacy Policy as needed when new builds introduce features or change how information is handled.

Before public release, TOS Creations will perform a final privacy policy review based on the application's release features and data practices.

Material changes will be reflected in the policy when they take effect. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.

The effective date will be revised when updates are published.
