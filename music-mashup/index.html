Welcome to Music Mashup Support

Music Mashup is a creative sound lab for turning songs and audio you own or have permission to use into custom mixes, transitions, and mashups.

This page provides help with importing audio, editing, previewing, exporting, and reporting problems.

Getting Started

Importing Your Audio

Use the Files app to select audio files you own or have permission to use. Music Mashup supports common formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, and AIFF.

Apple Music Library

You can browse, listen to, and queue supported Apple Music content. Apple Music tracks cannot be edited, mixed, or exported through Music Mashup.

Creating a Mix

Import two audio tracks to begin a quick blend. Arrange clips, trim start and end points, and adjust supported tempo and pitch controls.

Two-Deck Editor

Use the two-deck editor to work with two tracks together. Preview each deck, control clip overlap, and shape transitions without constantly changing screens.

Crossfades and Transitions

Adjust crossfades and overlapping clips to create smoother transitions. Preview the result before saving or exporting.

Saving Your Projects

Save your mixing projects so you can return later. Organize your queues and revisit your creative work using the available project controls.

Exporting a Finished Mix

Music Mashup supports WAV export for eligible imported audio. Exporting is limited to audio you own or have permission to use.

Apple Music tracks cannot be included in an exported mix.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I edit or export an Apple Music song?

Apple Music content is available for browsing, listening, and queueing only. Editing and exporting are restricted to audio you own or have permission to use.

Why can't I hear my audio?

Check your device volume and audio output. Make sure sound is not being routed to an unexpected Bluetooth or external audio device. If the problem continues, contact support.

What if an audio file won't import?

Check that the file is accessible through Files and is in a supported format. If it still fails, report the file format and the error you received. Please do not send copyrighted audio files to support.

What if an exported WAV file sounds wrong?

Check your clip selections, trimming, tempo, pitch, and crossfade settings. When reporting an issue, describe the expected result and how the actual export differed.

What if the app freezes or closes unexpectedly?

Please include your device model, app version, the screen you were using, and the steps that caused the problem.
