Privacy Policy

Music Mashup | TOS Creations

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

Music Mashup is developed by TOS Creations. We respect your creative work and recognize the importance of protecting your audio files, music projects, and personal information.

This policy explains how Music Mashup accesses, processes, stores, synchronizes, and manages information when you use the application.

Information Stored on Your Device

Music Mashup stores imported audio and related project information in local application storage.

This may include:

  • Imported audio files and filenames
  • Audio track titles and display names
  • Saved music projects and notes
  • Queues and clip arrangements
  • Clip start and end positions
  • Tempo, pitch, speed, and preset settings
  • Crossfade and transition settings
  • Project identifiers and creation dates
  • Favorites and playback preferences

This information supports editing, playback, project management, and music creation.

Imported Audio Files

When you import audio using Apple's Files interface, Music Mashup copies the selected file into its local application storage.

Supported audio formats include MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, AIF, and AIFF.

The application uses generated identifiers for internally stored files while retaining filenames for display and organization.

Imported audio may also be synchronized through Apple's private iCloud database, as described below.

iCloud and CloudKit Synchronization

Music Mashup uses Apple's CloudKit service to synchronize supported audio files, saved projects, queues, and editing settings.

When synchronization runs, imported audio files and associated metadata may be uploaded to the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account.

Synchronized information may include project snapshots, track metadata, imported audio files, clip settings, queue arrangements, and identifiers.

Synchronization may run during application startup and after changes to imported audio or supported project information.

An Apple Account with appropriate iCloud access is needed for cloud synchronization.

TOS Creations does not operate a separate company audio-upload server or custom database for this synchronization.

Information processed through iCloud and CloudKit is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Apple Music and Music Library Access

Music Mashup uses Apple's MusicKit and media-library services to provide music browsing, listening, favorites, and queueing.

With your authorization, the application may access music metadata such as song titles, artists, genres, durations, artwork, library identifiers, and dates added.

Apple Music services may communicate with Apple for authorization and playback.

Music Mashup does not directly send Apple Music library metadata to a separate TOS Creations server.

Apple Music tracks are available for browsing, listening, and queueing only. They cannot be edited, mixed, or exported through Music Mashup.

Music Editing and WAV Exports

Music Mashup processes eligible imported audio to create mixes, transitions, and WAV exports.

Temporary audio files may be created during export and are removed by the application's normal export process.

The application uses the system file exporter to let you choose where your completed WAV file will be saved.

Files saved to iCloud Drive, another cloud service, or a different external location are subject to that provider's storage and privacy practices.

Editing and exporting are intended for audio you own or have permission to use.

Accounts and Authentication

Music Mashup does not currently require a separate TOS Creations account, email registration, or application password.

Access to Apple Music and iCloud features may depend on your Apple Account, authorizations, and supported Apple services.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current Music Mashup application does not include developer-integrated advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Music Mashup does not use advertising identifiers to track users across apps or websites.

Apple's platform and services may process their own operational information according to Apple's applicable privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local application information generally remains available while the application and its stored data are retained.

Deleting Music Mashup normally removes its local application storage, including imported audio and locally saved projects.

Removing the application does not automatically delete previously synchronized iCloud records.

Saved projects, queues, metadata, and imported audio may remain in your private iCloud storage after uninstalling the app.

Deleting an individual saved project or imported audio track through the app initiates the corresponding supported CloudKit deletion process.

Cloud deletion depends on successful synchronization and the availability of Apple's iCloud services.

Exported files saved outside Music Mashup remain in their selected destination until separately deleted.

Users may also manage applicable iCloud information through Apple's account and device storage controls.

Information Sharing

Music Mashup does not currently upload imported audio or saved projects to a separate TOS Creations backend.

Supported content is synchronized through Apple's private CloudKit database.

Music Mashup does not include a public social feed that publishes users' imported audio or saved projects.

When you choose to export and share a finished file, that action is controlled through your selected destination or sharing service.

Security

Music Mashup uses Apple's application storage, file-access mechanisms, and CloudKit services for its supported storage and synchronization features.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

We encourage users to protect their Apple Account credentials and devices.

Children's Privacy

Music Mashup is not specifically directed toward children under 13.

The application does not currently offer separate TOS Creations account registration or directly request contact information from users.

Contact TOS Creations if you have questions about children's privacy.

Privacy Policy Updates and Beta Development

Music Mashup is currently undergoing beta testing and development.

TOS Creations will review and update this Privacy Policy as necessary when new builds introduce features or change how information is accessed, stored, synchronized, or shared.

Before public release, TOS Creations will perform a final privacy policy review based on the application's release features and data practices.

Material changes will be reflected in the policy when they take effect. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.

The effective date will be revised when changes are published.
