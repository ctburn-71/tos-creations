Welcome to My Books Support

My Books is a creative space for turning your memories, ideas, and stories into personalized digital books.

Whether you're creating a family scrapbook, journal, cookbook, baby book, or collection of special moments, we're here to help.

Getting Started

Creating Your First Book

Open My Books and select "Create Your Own Book." Enter a title, choose your book style and customization options, and create your book.

Adding Photos

Open a book and select Photo to add an image from your photo library, or Camera to capture a new memory.

Adding and Editing Text

Select Text from the page toolbar to add words to your pages. Use the editing controls to customize the appearance and placement.

Adding More Pages

Select Page from the editing toolbar to add another page to your book.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create more than one book?

Yes! My Books allows you to create and organize multiple books on your personal bookshelf.

Does My Books support iCloud?

My Books supports synchronization through Apple's iCloud services when available and configured on your device.

Can I customize my pages?

Yes. You can add text, photos, and other available creative elements to personalize your pages.

What if my books are not syncing?

Check that you're signed in to iCloud, have an internet connection, and have iCloud access enabled for My Books.

If the problem continues, contact support. Please avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting synchronization problems.
