Welcome to Fitness OS Support

Fitness OS helps you build training routines, log workouts, track progress, and explore personalized AI-supported coaching. We're here to help you get the most from your experience.

Getting Started

Account Access

Create an account in Fitness OS to get started. If you have trouble signing in, use the password-reset option or contact support.

My Routines

Create reusable workouts, customize exercise targets, and return to saved routines whenever you're ready.

Boot Camp

Choose guided sessions with estimated durations of 20, 30, or 45 minutes. Boot Camp is included with Fitness OS at no extra charge.

Workout Tracking

Log completed sets, repetitions, weights, and timed holds where supported. Review your completed activity in workout history.

AI Coach

Ask questions about workouts, training goals, and progress. AI coaching has a usage allowance. Core workout logging, routines, and history remain available when that allowance is reached.

AI advice may be inaccurate and does not replace qualified medical or fitness professionals.

Practice Recordings

Save and review private practice videos within the application. Automated video form analysis is not currently available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Boot Camp included?

Yes. Boot Camp is included at no extra charge.

What happens if I reach my AI allowance?

AI coaching may be limited until additional usage becomes available. Your saved workouts, routines, and history remain accessible.

What should I do if workout data is missing?

Avoid repeatedly creating the same workout entry. Contact support with your app version, device model, and a description of the issue.
