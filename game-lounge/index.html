Welcome to the Game Lounge

The Dart Club & Game Lounge brings four distinct games together in one entertainment experience. Each game features its own atmosphere, controls, and challenges.

Whether you're aiming for a bullseye, lining up a pool shot, challenging the computer at air hockey, or hitting the bowling lanes, we want every game to feel enjoyable and responsive.

Explore the Four Games

🎯 Darts

Sharpen your aim, test your accuracy, and enjoy The Dart Club experience. Online competition is planned as the community grows.

🎱 Pool

Enjoy a billiards experience focused on aiming, ball movement, scoring, and gameplay. Online play options are part of the experience.

🏒 Air Hockey

Challenge a computer opponent and test your reaction time. Switch between classic gameplay and the optional Midnight Neon atmosphere.

🎳 Bowling

Step onto the lanes for a classic bowling experience, or activate Midnight Neon mode for an after-dark bowling atmosphere.

What to Test

During TestFlight testing, please explore the following features:

  • All four games: Darts, Pool, Bowling, and Air Hockey.
  • Controls: Aiming, dragging, shooting, and responsiveness.
  • Scoring: Points, turns, wins, and restarting games.
  • Sound: Effects, volume settings, and mute.
  • Navigation: Opening games, returning home, switching lounges, and changing settings.
  • Midnight Neon: Switch between classic and neon environments in Air Hockey and Bowling. Check visuals and gameplay in both modes.
  • Display and performance: Text that doesn't fit, overlapping buttons, slowdowns, freezes, and crashes.
  • Online play, when available: Create or join a match, test turn order, score synchronization, matchmaking, and reconnection.

Having Trouble?

If something isn't working correctly, please try closing and reopening the game. Make sure your device is running a supported operating system and that you're using the latest available TestFlight build.

For online features, check your internet connection before retrying.

If the problem continues, please send us a description of what happened.

Report an Issue or Share Feedback

Feedback helps improve The Dart Club & Game Lounge before public release.

When reporting an issue, please include your device model, the game you were playing, what happened, and the steps needed to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are helpful when available.
