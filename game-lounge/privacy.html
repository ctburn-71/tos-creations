Privacy Policy

The Dart Club & Game Lounge

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

The Dart Club & Game Lounge is developed and provided by TOS Creations.

We respect your privacy and believe you should understand how your information is handled when enjoying our games.

This policy covers Darts, Pool, Air Hockey, Bowling, and the private online multiplayer features currently available in the application.

Information You Provide

The application does not require you to provide an email address, password, phone number, or real name to play.

You may choose a Club nickname. This nickname is saved locally and may be shared with another player during a private online Darts match.

Please avoid using personal information in your nickname that you do not want another player to see.

Information Stored on Your Device

The application stores certain game information locally to remember your preferences and progress.

This may include:

  • Club nickname and appearance preferences.
  • Achievements, rewards, and unlocks.
  • Personal best scores and daily challenges.
  • Gameplay settings and preferences.
  • Midnight Neon and lighting selections.
  • Locally saved testing notes and feedback.

Testing notes saved inside the app are not automatically submitted to TOS Creations. You may choose to copy and share them.

Depending on your device settings, operating-system backup or transfer services may back up and restore locally saved information. The app does not provide a separate account-based cloud synchronization service for these records.

Private Online Darts

Private online Darts uses Google Firebase services to connect players and operate matches.

Firebase Authentication generates a guest account identifier for online play. You are not required to create a traditional email-and-password account.

Online match information may include:

  • Generated guest player identifiers.
  • Chosen player names.
  • Private room codes and match status.
  • Scores, turns, throws, and results.
  • Match creation and update information.

This information is processed and stored using Firebase Authentication, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Firestore.

Your opponent can see your chosen player name and relevant match information. The current online mode does not provide free-text chat, voice chat, or file sharing.

Pool, Air Hockey, and Bowling do not use the private online Darts service in the current inspected version.

Third-Party Services

The application uses certain services to support its functionality.

Google Firebase

Firebase Authentication, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Firestore provide guest authentication, private match operations, and online data storage.

Google may process technical connection information such as IP addresses, request metadata, and related information for service operation, reliability, and security.

Google Fonts

When fonts are not available locally, the application may download them from Google servers. These requests involve standard network connection information.

Apple TestFlight

During beta testing, Apple may collect usage information, crash reports, and tester feedback and make certain information available to the developer.

Third-party services also operate under their own applicable privacy terms.

Advertising and Tracking

The current inspected application does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics integrations, or cross-app advertising tracking.

We will update this policy if these practices change in future versions.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate gameplay, remember settings and progress, authenticate private online sessions, synchronize match results, support service security, and respond to user feedback or support requests.

We do not currently use the game's collected online match information for personalized advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game records generally remain until replaced, cleared through device storage controls, or removed with application data. Device backups may retain separate copies.

Online Darts guest accounts and match records are stored using Firebase. Leaving a match, closing the application, or reaching a room's gameplay expiration does not guarantee that associated online records are immediately deleted.

Retention may vary by record type and service. Infrastructure logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules.

If you have questions about your online information or wish to request deletion, contact us at toscreations71@gmail.com .

We will review requests and may need to verify account ownership before acting on online records. An in-app guest-account deletion feature is not yet available in this beta version.

Children's Privacy

The game does not require players to provide a date of birth or traditional registration information.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to help younger players choose nicknames that do not reveal personal details.

If you have concerns about information relating to a child, please contact TOS Creations using the address below.

Security

Online gameplay uses HTTPS connections and Firebase guest authentication. Match access is subject to game-service authorization checks.

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by the app, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

The Dart Club & Game Lounge is actively being developed and tested.

As features, online services, or information-handling practices change, this privacy policy may be updated.

Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact TOS Creations

For privacy questions, support requests, or concerns about your information, contact us directly.
