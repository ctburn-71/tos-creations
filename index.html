My Books — Your Stories, Your Way

Turn your memories, ideas, recipes, travels, and everyday moments into beautiful personal books.

My Books makes it easy to create and organize custom digital books with your own titles, text, photos, colors, fonts, and page styles. Start with a blank book or choose an inspiring idea from the bookshelf, then make it yours.

Create Books for Every Occasion

Create cookbooks, journals, scrapbooks, travel memories, family storybooks, wedding books, and more.

Make Every Page Your Own

Add photos from your library, write and edit text, move and resize items, rotate images, and arrange each page to match your vision.

Personalize Your Bookshelf

Choose different book colors, fonts, flat covers, or spine-out designs. Your books are displayed together in a warm, personal library that grows with you.

Preserve What Matters

Whether you're preserving family memories, collecting favorite recipes, planning an adventure, or capturing your thoughts, My Books gives you a creative place to make each story your own.

  • Make changes to text, photos, and backgrounds.
  • Leave the book, then reopen it.
  • Confirm all content and formatting remain intact.
  • Check positions, sizes, rotations, colors, and backgrounds.

Crossword Jigsaw

Solve the words. Reveal the picture.

Crossword Jigsaw brings word clues and picture puzzles together in one relaxing challenge. Solve clues to unlock pieces, then fit them into place to reveal beautiful, realistic artwork.

Enjoy a quiet puzzle break or give yourself a challenge. Choose your difficulty, play at your own pace, and turn on the optional timer when you want to test your speed.

Two Ways to Exercise Your Mind

Use your word knowledge to solve clues and your visual skills to assemble the picture. Each answer brings you closer to the finished image.

Beautiful Worlds to Discover

Explore artwork inspired by ancient wonders, coastal scenery, and the world beyond Earth. Watch each scene come together, one piece at a time.

A Challenge That Fits Your Mood

Choose Easy, Medium, or Expert puzzles. Add an extra twist with optional piece rotation, or keep things relaxed with untimed play.

Build Your Gallery

Pick Up Where You Left Off

Whether you love word games, jigsaws, or discovering something a little different, Crossword Jigsaw gives you a satisfying reason to find that next answer—and place that next piece.

What to Test

Thank you for testing Crossword Jigsaw! Please focus on:

  • Getting started: Is it clear how solving word clues unlocks jigsaw pieces?
  • Puzzle difficulty: Try Easy, Medium, and Expert. Are the clues enjoyable, appropriately challenging, and accurate?
  • Puzzle controls: Check selecting, moving, and placing pieces. Try optional piece rotation and the timer.
  • Saving progress: Leave a puzzle unfinished, close the app, and reopen it. Confirm your answers and placed pieces are restored.
  • Navigation: Move between Home, Puzzles, Gallery, and Settings. Report any confusing buttons or screens you cannot exit.
  • Artwork and gallery: Complete puzzles and confirm their pictures appear in your gallery. If you finish a full collection, try the unlocked master puzzle.
  • Downloads and offline play: Download available content, then try playing without an internet connection.
  • Display and performance: Watch for cut-off text, tiny controls, slow loading, freezes, or crashes.

We'd also love to hear what feels fun, what feels frustrating, and what would make you want to play again.

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, what you were doing, what happened, and a screenshot if possible.

Explore Music Mashup Details

Music Mashup Project

Music Mashup is a creative sound lab for turning songs and owned audio into custom mixes, transitions, and mashups.

Browse and play music from your Apple Music library, or import audio files you own from the Files app. Build a queue, arrange clips, trim start and end points, adjust tempo and pitch, add crossfades, preview your changes, and export your finished mix as a WAV file.

Two-Deck Editor

The two-deck editor makes it easier to shape transitions between songs without constantly moving between screens. Adjust both tracks together, preview each deck, control the overlap between clips, and hear your mix before exporting.

Supported Audio Formats

Music Mashup supports MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, and AIFF audio files.

Apple Music Integration

Apple Music content is available for listening, browsing, and queueing only. Apple Music tracks cannot be edited, mixed, or exported.

Editing and exporting are limited to audio that you own or have permission to use.

Create Something of Your Own

Create a quick blend from your first two imported tracks, save projects for later, organize your queues, and build something that sounds like yours.

Help Us Test Music Mashup

Please test the complete creative workflow.

Getting Started

  1. Launch the app and review the onboarding flow.
  2. Try the demo mix.
  3. Open Library and confirm Apple Music permission and library browsing.
  4. Tap an Apple Music song to start playback, tap it again to pause, and try another song.
  5. Add an Apple Music track to the playback queue.
  6. Confirm Apple Music tracks remain playback-only and cannot be edited or exported.

Importing and Editing Audio

  1. Open Owned Audio and import MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, or AIFF files from Files.
  2. Add at least two imported tracks to the queue.
  3. Open Imported Audio Queue.
  4. Use the “Edit both tracks together” editor.
  5. Adjust Deck A and Deck B start/end sliders.
  6. Adjust the transition/crossfade length.
  7. Preview each deck and confirm playback can start and stop.
  8. Play the edited queue and verify the changes are audible.
  9. Test tempo/playback-rate and pitch adjustments.
  10. Reorder clips, delete a clip, and add the same clip more than once.

Mixing, Exporting and Projects

  1. Use Quick Mix with two imported tracks.
  2. Export the queue as a WAV file and confirm the file can be saved and played.
  3. Create, rename, and delete a project. Confirm the delete confirmation appears.
  4. Test the iCloud sync button and confirm the app remains usable if sync is unavailable.

Reliability and Device Testing

  1. Test the app after resetting simulator state with --reset-state.
  2. Check scrolling, tab navigation, text readability, and controls on a physical device.

TOS Retro Games

Step inside the neon grid with a five-level retro arcade stacking challenge designed to test timing, reflexes, balance, and spatial strategy.

Inspired by classic arcade games, TOS Retro Games combines simple controls with increasingly demanding challenges, glowing visual effects, and an adaptive synthesized soundtrack.

Level 1 — Classic Slice

Drop sliding blocks onto your tower. Overhanging sections are sliced away, shrinking your platform with every miss. Precision is the key to building higher.

Level 2 — The Balance Beam

Blocks retain their full size. Gravity and weight distribution determine whether your tower remains standing. Stack too far off-center and watch it tilt.

Level 3 — Grid-Fit Puzzle

Fit fractional pieces of 25%, 50%, and 75% together to complete a full row without gaps. Every placement matters.

Level 4 — The Shift Grid

The target landing area moves left and right while blocks slide overhead. Track the movement and time your drops carefully.

Level 5 — Blind Neon

The grid disappears into darkness, briefly flashing in time with the retro soundtrack. Rely on rhythm, memory, and precise timing.

Arcade Features

  • Five distinct arcade challenges
  • Local Top 5 leaderboards
  • Three-letter initials for high scores
  • Streak multipliers and bonus points
  • Adaptive synthesized arcade audio
  • Daily challenges and personal achievements
  • Skyline, medal, and progression systems

Five levels. Endless opportunities to beat your personal best.

My Music OS

Turn your phone into a pocket dual-deck DJ booth. Load two tracks side by side, blend them with a crossfader, and explore music creation through hands-on controls and a glowing, audio-reactive interface.

Dual-Deck Mixing

Control two decks with EQ, pitch adjustments, crossfading, hot cues, and loop controls. Blend music and create your own transitions.

Camelot Key-Matching Wheel

Explore harmonic mixing using an interactive Camelot wheel. The display highlights each deck's musical key and helps you identify compatible combinations.

Smart Sync and Tempo Detection

Use on-device tempo and musical-key analysis with the included studio loops. Smart Sync aligns supported loops to a common beat grid, while a metronome provides additional timing help.

Studio Loops

Five built-in studio loops let you begin mixing immediately, including the Velvet Strings guitar loop.

Library and Crates

Import audio from your device, organize tracks into named crates, and load them onto either deck.

Recording

Capture and export supported mixes created from the built-in studio loops, then save or share your recordings.

Recording currently does not support mixes containing imported device tracks.

Studio Coach and Achievements

Learn the controls using the built-in Studio Coach and earn achievements as you explore mixing, synchronization, and harmonic matching.

Mix creatively. Learn the controls. Build your sound.

Help Us Test My Music OS

Please explore the complete DJ mixing experience, including the latest guitar loop, Camelot wheel, library organization, and core audio controls.

1. Dual-Deck Audio and Mixer

  • Load studio loops onto Deck A and Deck B.
  • Start playback and check that the decks stay synchronized.
  • Move the crossfader from left to right and listen for smooth transitions.
  • Test volume and EQ controls for crackling, popping, clipping, or silence.
  • Try pitch controls, hot cues, and loops.
  • Test Smart Sync and the metronome.
  • Check mute and solo controls where available.

2. Velvet Strings Guitar Loop

  • Open Studio and load Velvet Strings onto either deck.
  • Confirm audio plays clearly.
  • Check its detected BPM and musical key.
  • Try Smart Sync with another studio loop.
  • Test its hot cues and loop controls.
  • Compare its volume and sound quality with the other studio loops.

3. Camelot Wheel

  • Load different studio loops onto Deck A and Deck B.
  • Check that each deck's key appears in its designated color.
  • Look for neighboring key indicators.
  • Confirm matching keys produce the green highlight and white ring.
  • Compare the wheel with the text readout below it.
  • Tell us whether key matching feels clear or confusing.

4. Imported Audio and Crates

  • Import an audio file using Load Track.
  • Confirm the imported track appears in Your Imports.
  • Reload it onto either deck from the Library.
  • Create a named crate and organize tracks inside it.
  • Rename and delete a crate.
  • Confirm deleting a crate doesn't delete its tracks.
  • Delete an imported track and confirm its library and crate references disappear.

5. Recording and Sharing

  • Load supported built-in studio loops.
  • Record a mix using the available recording controls.
  • Save or share the finished WAV recording.
  • Confirm the recording can be played back.
  • Check how recording behaves when imported tracks are loaded.

6. Reliability and Navigation

  • Test scrolling and control responsiveness.
  • Background the app during playback, then return.
  • Close and reopen the app to check saved session settings.
  • Explore Studio Coach and confirm its explanations are clear.
  • Check readability, screen layouts, and accessibility.
  • Report crashes, freezes, audio glitches, or unexpected behavior.

Fitness OS — Build a Better You

Fitness OS helps you build a training routine that fits your goals, experience, and available time.

Create reusable workouts in My Routines, choose a guided Boot Camp, or explore individual exercises. Boot Camp is included with Fitness OS at no extra charge and offers estimated 20-, 30-, and 45-minute sessions with exercises to review before you begin.

Record completed sets, repetitions, weights, and timed holds where supported. Pause and resume a workout, adjust supported exercises, or end a session early while keeping a record of what you completed. Workout history and progress tracking help you reflect on your training over time.

The AI Coach offers personalized training guidance using the goals, preferences, and workout information you share with Fitness OS. Ask questions, discuss how a session felt, and explore possible next steps. AI coaching has a usage allowance; your routines, workout logging, and history remain available when that allowance is used up.

You can also save private practice recordings and review them alongside your training. Automated video form analysis is not currently available.

Fitness OS is being developed for people with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of training experience. This beta will help us understand where the app feels clear and useful—and where it needs to improve.

AI guidance can be inaccurate and does not replace professional medical advice or an individualized assessment by a qualified trainer.

Build a better you, at your own pace.

AI Coach

Recordings and Reliability

  • Recording currently supports personal review, not automated form analysis.

  • Test password-reset email delivery.

Accessibility and Comfort

  • Are text and buttons readable and easy to use?
  • Try larger phone text settings or VoiceOver if you normally use them.
  • Tell us where instructions, exercise choices, or targets don't fit your needs. Don't push through discomfort for testing.

When Reporting Feedback

Include your app version/build number, iPhone model, what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. Screenshots are helpful, but avoid sharing passwords, reset codes, or private recordings.

Our highest priorities are crashes, missing or duplicated workout data, getting stuck, and unclear navigation.

About TOS Creations

TOS Creations is an independent app development brand built on creativity, curiosity, and the belief that technology should be both useful and personal.

What started with a passion for bringing ideas to life has grown into a collection of digital experiences designed to entertain, inspire, organize, and connect. From interactive games and music applications to fitness tools and creative scrapbooks.

Every project begins with a simple question:What if we could make this experience better, more enjoyable, or more meaningful? At TOS Creations, we believe the best ideas don't always come from following trends. Sometimes they come from everyday experiences, personal challenges, family moments, or simply imagining something that doesn't exist yet. Our approach is straightforward: build with purpose, pay attention to the details, keep learning, and never stop exploring what's possible. Some creations are designed to make life easier. Others are made purely for fun. And some are built to preserve the moments that matter most.
