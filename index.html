Crossword Jigsaw

Solve the words. Reveal the picture.

Crossword Jigsaw brings word clues and picture puzzles together in one relaxing challenge. Solve clues to unlock pieces, then fit them into place to reveal beautiful, realistic artwork.

Enjoy a quiet puzzle break or give yourself a challenge. Choose your difficulty, play at your own pace, and turn on the optional timer when you want to test your speed.

Two Ways to Exercise Your Mind

Use your word knowledge to solve clues and your visual skills to assemble the picture. Each answer brings you closer to the finished image.

Beautiful Worlds to Discover

Explore artwork inspired by ancient wonders, coastal scenery, and the world beyond Earth. Watch each scene come together, one piece at a time.

A Challenge That Fits Your Mood

Choose Easy, Medium, or Expert puzzles. Add an extra twist with optional piece rotation, or keep things relaxed with untimed play.

Build Your Gallery

Every completed puzzle becomes part of your collection. Complete a collection to unlock a larger master puzzle that brings your achievements together.

Pick Up Where You Left Off

Your progress saves automatically on your device, so you can step away and return to your unfinished puzzle.

Whether you love word games, jigsaws, or discovering something a little different, Crossword Jigsaw gives you a satisfying reason to find that next answer—and place that next piece.

Created by TOS Creations.

What to Test

Thank you for testing Crossword Jigsaw! Please focus on:

  • Getting started: Is it clear how solving word clues unlocks jigsaw pieces?
  • Puzzle difficulty: Try Easy, Medium, and Expert. Are the clues enjoyable, appropriately challenging, and accurate?
  • Puzzle controls: Check selecting, moving, and placing pieces. Try optional piece rotation and the timer.
  • Saving progress: Leave a puzzle unfinished, close the app, and reopen it. Confirm your answers and placed pieces are restored.
  • Navigation: Move between Home, Puzzles, Gallery, and Settings. Report any confusing buttons or screens you cannot exit.
  • Artwork and gallery: Complete puzzles and confirm their pictures appear in your gallery. If you finish a full collection, try the unlocked master puzzle.
  • Downloads and offline play: Download available content, then try playing without an internet connection.
  • Display and performance: Watch for cut-off text, tiny controls, slow loading, freezes, or crashes.

We'd also love to hear what feels fun, what feels frustrating, and what would make you want to play again.

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, what you were doing, what happened, and a screenshot if possible.

Music Mashup Project

Music Mashup is a creative sound lab for turning songs and owned audio into custom mixes, transitions, and mashups.

Browse and play music from your Apple Music library, or import audio files you own from the Files app. Build a queue, arrange clips, trim start and end points, adjust tempo and pitch, add crossfades, preview your changes, and export your finished mix as a WAV file.

Two-Deck Editor

The two-deck editor makes it easier to shape transitions between songs without constantly moving between screens. Adjust both tracks together, preview each deck, control the overlap between clips, and hear your mix before exporting.

Supported Audio Formats

Music Mashup supports MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, and AIFF audio files.

Apple Music Integration

Apple Music content is available for listening, browsing, and queueing only. Apple Music tracks cannot be edited, mixed, or exported.

Editing and exporting are limited to audio that you own or have permission to use.

Create Something of Your Own

Create a quick blend from your first two imported tracks, save projects for later, organize your queues, and build something that sounds like yours.

Help Us Test Music Mashup

Please test the complete creative workflow.

Getting Started

  1. Launch the app and review the onboarding flow.
  2. Try the demo mix.
  3. Open Library and confirm Apple Music permission and library browsing.
  4. Tap an Apple Music song to start playback, tap it again to pause, and try another song.
  5. Add an Apple Music track to the playback queue.
  6. Confirm Apple Music tracks remain playback-only and cannot be edited or exported.

Importing and Editing Audio

  1. Open Owned Audio and import MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, or AIFF files from Files.
  2. Add at least two imported tracks to the queue.
  3. Open Imported Audio Queue.
  4. Use the “Edit both tracks together” editor.
  5. Adjust Deck A and Deck B start/end sliders.
  6. Adjust the transition/crossfade length.
  7. Preview each deck and confirm playback can start and stop.
  8. Play the edited queue and verify the changes are audible.
  9. Test tempo/playback-rate and pitch adjustments.
  10. Reorder clips, delete a clip, and add the same clip more than once.

Mixing, Exporting and Projects

  1. Use Quick Mix with two imported tracks.
  2. Export the queue as a WAV file and confirm the file can be saved and played.
  3. Create, rename, and delete a project. Confirm the delete confirmation appears.
  4. Test the iCloud sync button and confirm the app remains usable if sync is unavailable.

Reliability and Device Testing

  1. Test the app after resetting simulator state with --reset-state.
  2. Check scrolling, tab navigation, text readability, and controls on a physical device.

Fitness OS — Build a Better You

Fitness OS helps you build a training routine that fits your goals, experience, and available time.

Create reusable workouts in My Routines, choose a guided Boot Camp, or explore individual exercises. Boot Camp is included with Fitness OS at no extra charge and offers estimated 20-, 30-, and 45-minute sessions with exercises to review before you begin.

Record completed sets, repetitions, weights, and timed holds where supported. Pause and resume a workout, adjust supported exercises, or end a session early while keeping a record of what you completed. Workout history and progress tracking help you reflect on your training over time.

The AI Coach offers personalized training guidance using the goals, preferences, and workout information you share with Fitness OS. Ask questions, discuss how a session felt, and explore possible next steps. AI coaching has a usage allowance; your routines, workout logging, and history remain available when that allowance is used up.

You can also save private practice recordings and review them alongside your training. Automated video form analysis is not currently available.

Fitness OS is being developed for people with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of training experience. This beta will help us understand where the app feels clear and useful—and where it needs to improve.

AI guidance can be inaccurate and does not replace professional medical advice or an individualized assessment by a qualified trainer.

Build a better you, at your own pace.

Help Us Test Fitness OS

Thank you for helping test Fitness OS. We want to learn whether the app is understandable and useful for people with different experience levels and abilities. You do not need to complete a demanding workout to provide valuable feedback.

First Impressions and Navigation

  • Create an account and explore before asking for help.
  • Is it obvious where to start a workout?
  • Can you explain the difference between My Routines, Boot Camp, and the Exercise Library?
  • Is it clear that Boot Camp is included at no extra charge?
  • Note any screen where you feel lost or unsure what to tap next.

Workout Flow

  • Create a short routine, edit its targets, and reorder exercises.
  • Review a Boot Camp and explore available exercise swaps.
  • Log sets, repetitions, weights, or timed holds where available.
  • Pause, switch tabs, and find your workout again.
  • Finish a workout—or end early—and check that your completed work appears correctly in history.
  • Return later and repeat a saved routine.

AI Coach

  • Ask about choosing a workout, adjusting an exercise, or understanding your progress.
  • Check whether responses reflect the information you actually provided.
  • Report confusing, inaccurate, overly confident, or unsuitable advice.
  • Is the AI usage allowance easy to understand?

Recordings and Reliability

  • If comfortable, record a short practice clip, save it, play it back, and delete it.
  • Recording currently supports personal review, not automated form analysis.
  • Try closing and reopening the app during a workout.
  • If a connection fails, check whether retrying works without losing or duplicating records.
  • Test password-reset email delivery.

Accessibility and Comfort

  • Are text and buttons readable and easy to use?
  • Try larger phone text settings or VoiceOver if you normally use them.
  • Tell us where instructions, exercise choices, or targets don't fit your needs. Don't push through discomfort for testing.

When Reporting Feedback

Include your app version/build number, iPhone model, what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. Screenshots are helpful, but avoid sharing passwords, reset codes, or private recordings.

Our highest priorities are crashes, missing or duplicated workout data, getting stuck, and unclear navigation.
